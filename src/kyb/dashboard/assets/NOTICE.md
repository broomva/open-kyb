# Material provenance

Orb shader, ASCII flow and chrome/material grammar adapted from Liquid Midnight,
Apache-2.0, skills repository commit `7730d0f2dc86ab2ee9ec4b4cb9674c59c1505a3f`,
`skills/design/liquid-midnight/assets/`. Original kit derives from
broomva/agent-workspaces `187972f`. open-kyb hue adaptation follows the owner's approved
design and `docs/visual/design-system.md`.

Local Raleway, Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts retain the OFL notices in `fonts/`.
No remote font, script, analytics or provider requests are made by this UI.
